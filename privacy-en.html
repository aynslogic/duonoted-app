Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026 · DuoNoted (package net.duonoted.app)

General

DuoNoted does not require a user account and does not request identifying information (name, email address, payment details). Your texts, books, and cards are stored only on your device. The app relies on limited third-party services for translation and advertising. This policy may be updated; material changes will be communicated within the app.

Translation

In the following cases, the selected text is sent to Google's translation service: automatic translation on the Texts step, and looking up the meaning of a word or phrase while reading. These requests are sent directly from your device to Google; DuoNoted never sees, processes, or stores this data — so there is nothing on our side to delete. How long Google retains this data is governed by Google's own policies.

Ads

The app displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address/approximate location, and device information for ad delivery and measurement. For details: how Google uses data in advertising.

What's stored on your device

Drafts, books, cards, and preferences are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time via Settings → Your data → "Delete all data," or by uninstalling the app.

Your rights

We hold no data that identifies you. Users located in the European Union/EEA have the right to access, correct, or delete their data, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — however, we hold no data record for these rights to apply to. Requests regarding ad data must be directed to Google.

DuoNoted is not directed at users under the age of 13.

Contact

For any privacy question or request: aynslogic@gmail.com
